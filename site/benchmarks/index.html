Every result below names its release, hardware lane, and measurement boundary. Runtime qualification and public-route acceptance are distinct, and warm/cold figures compare retained state against a fresh cold start, never an ordinary in-process cached follow-up.

Historical restart measurements: v0.4.0 (2026-08-30, one RTX 5090)

Historical v0.4.0 proof on one RTX 5090 (2026-08-30): 109,589 retained tokens served after a Docker restart in 24.8 s end-to-end including first-touch restoration of a 7.95 GB checkpoint, with a 56.6 s model reload measured separately and 0.778 s server-side time to first token after restoration (restart receipt). A separate v0.4.0 request pair recorded 47.920 s cold wall time versus 1.790 s warm follow-up wall time against a fresh cold start (separate request-pair receipt). These historical samples use distinct timing boundaries and are not current-release latency promises or a restart speed-up ratio.
